Over Ons

Jorge Vazquez Dance Productions is a premier dance school located in the heart of Amsterdam, bringing passion, rhythm, and top-tier instruction to the local dance community. Led by experienced professionals, the school specializes in dynamic dance styles, offering expert guidance for students of all levels ranging from beginners to advanced dancers.

With a strong commitment to technique, musicality, and a welcoming atmosphere, Jorge Vazquez Dance Productions provides an inspiring environment where dancers can grow, connect, and experience the joy of movement.
